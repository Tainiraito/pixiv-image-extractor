# v1.4.0

[简体中文](#简体中文) · [English](#english)

## 简体中文

本次更新带来后台下载、可暂停的任务管理和更紧凑的弹窗。关闭弹窗或切换标签页后，下载仍可继续。

### 新增

- 后台原图下载与 ZIP 自动分卷，按图片张数和容量拆分大型作品。
- 本地任务历史，支持暂停、继续和仅重试未完成部分；最多保留 50 条记录，满额自动清理最早成功完成的记录。
- 顶部任务列表入口与下载设置，ZIP 每卷张数可用滑块按 1 张调整。

### 改进

- 图片预览默认三行，超过九张可展开全部，收起后保留选择。
- 首页最多显示三条未完成任务，完成后自动移入任务列表；任务卡片精简为两行。
- 作品信息与入口吸顶，优化下载按钮文案与滚动条；任务列表仅内容区域滚动。

### 修复

- 多窗口同时启动任务时，可能互相中断下载。
- 任务启动过程中重开弹窗，可能错误地显示中断并丢失进度。
- 浏览器下载暂停后，停止任务可能一直无法完成。

### 升级提示

**最低 Chrome 版本从 v1.2 的 88 提升至 116。**升级前请确认浏览器版本。

下载 [pixiv-image-extractor-v1.4.0.zip](https://github.com/Tainiraito/pixiv-image-extractor/releases/download/v1.4.0/pixiv-image-extractor-v1.4.0.zip)，解压后覆盖原扩展目录，再到 `chrome://extensions/` 点击「重新加载」并刷新 Pixiv 页面。首次安装请开启「开发者模式」，通过「加载已解压的扩展程序」选择解压后的文件夹。

感谢 [@KidsZZ](https://github.com/KidsZZ) 在 [#1](https://github.com/Tainiraito/pixiv-image-extractor/pull/1) 中贡献后台下载与自动分卷功能。

## English

This release adds background downloads, pausable task management, and a more compact popup. Downloads continue after closing the popup or switching tabs.

### Added

- Background downloads of original images and automatic ZIP splitting by image count and size.
- Local task history with pause, resume, and retries for unfinished items. Up to 50 records are retained; the oldest successful record is removed when the limit is reached and a new task is created.
- Task-list and download-settings entry points at the top, with a ZIP image-count slider adjustable in steps of 1.

### Changed

- The gallery initially shows three rows. Expand collections of more than nine images; collapsing preserves selections.
- The main view shows up to three unfinished tasks. Successful tasks move to the full list, with compact two-line task cards.
- Artwork details and entry buttons stay visible while scrolling. Download labels and scrollbars are simplified, and only the content area of the task-list dialog scrolls.

### Fixed

- Starting tasks from multiple windows could interrupt another download.
- Reopening the popup during startup could incorrectly mark a task as interrupted and discard progress.
- Stopping a task could hang when its browser download was paused.

### Upgrading

**The minimum Chrome version increases from 88 in v1.2 to 116.**Check your browser version before updating.

Download [pixiv-image-extractor-v1.4.0.zip](https://github.com/Tainiraito/pixiv-image-extractor/releases/download/v1.4.0/pixiv-image-extractor-v1.4.0.zip), extract it over the existing extension folder, click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions/`, and refresh Pixiv pages. For a first installation, enable **Developer mode** and choose the extracted folder through **Load unpacked**.

Thanks to [@KidsZZ](https://github.com/KidsZZ) for contributing background downloads and automatic ZIP splitting in [#1](https://github.com/Tainiraito/pixiv-image-extractor/pull/1).
